SUBJECT: Placement of Constitution (26th Amend.) Bill
1.

The Ministry of Law and Justice, through Cabinet Secretariat U.O. No. 4(12)/2024-Min-I dated 23rd October 2024, has transmitted the official Government Bill entitled "The Constitution (Twenty-Sixth Amendment) Act, 2024" with the formal recommendation of the Federal Cabinet under Article 74 of the 1973 Constitution.

2.

The Bill primarily envisions institutional reform regarding the selection and evaluation framework of the superior judiciary under Article 175A, proposing an integrated Parliamentary Committee representation and structural benchmarks for high court appointments.

3.

In accordance with Rule 123 of the Rules of Procedure and Conduct of Business in the National Assembly 2007, notice of three clear days is routinely mandated unless the Speaker dispenses with this requirement in exercise of discretion under proviso to Rule 123(1).

Legislative Branch Observation: Leader of the Opposition has orally requested 48 hours for caucus review before formal introduction.
4.

Submitted for appropriate orders regarding placement of the legislative business on the Orders of the Day for the forthcoming sitting scheduled for Friday, 25th October 2024 at 10:30 AM.
