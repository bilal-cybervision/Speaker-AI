Government of Pakistan

National Assembly Secretariat

Legislation & Finance Wing (Budget Branch)

F. No. 14(3)/2024-Legis. Islamabad, the 23rd October, 2024
Subject: Proposal for modernizing audio-visual recording, Hansard verbatim transcription, and AI-enabled speech translation in Hall of the Assembly.

Para 12. Reference directions issued on 14th August 2024 during the inter-wing strategic modernization committee. The existing analogue audio recording consoles and Hansard shorthand capture infrastructure, installed under the 13th Assembly tenure in 2011, have exceeded their operational lifespan. Frequent technical lags and non-availability of OEM micro-components require an immediate comprehensive modernization.

Para 13. The Joint Parliamentary IT Board along with the Directorate General of Media & Communications have finalized the revised specifications for multi-channel digital audio capture, real-time AI assistive transcripts in bilingual format (English and Urdu), and redundancy archiving within the National Assembly private cloud servers.

Para 14. The Finance Division has officially concurred via U.O. No. 412-FD/Exp-II/2024 dated 19-10-2024 for re-appropriation of PKR 48,200,000/- (Forty-Eight Million Two Hundred Thousand Pakistani Rupees) from Demand No. 008 (National Assembly), Object Code "A03970 - Others" to "A09200 - Purchase of Machinery & Technical Equipment". Consequently, no net additional budgetary allocation will be solicited from the federal consolidated fund.

Para 15. Procurement shall strictly adhere to Rule 42(c) of the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) Rules 2004, through competitive open tendering with sovereign vendor evaluation benchmarks vetted by the Special Evaluation Panel.

Para 16. Submitted for the gracious approval of the Honorable Speaker, National Assembly of Pakistan under Rule 19 of the National Assembly Rules of Procedure & Conduct of Business, 2007.
